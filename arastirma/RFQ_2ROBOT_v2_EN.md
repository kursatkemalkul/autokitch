# TURNKEY MACHINE — REQUEST FOR QUOTATION
**Automated flatbread kiosk (pizza / pide / lahmacun) — two robots, no conveyor, cavity ovens. v2 (16.09.2026)**
Torinoarch Studio · Istanbul, Turkey

*v2 change vs v1: no conveyor anywhere in the machine; baking is done in lidded cavity ovens loaded by the robot, so each product bakes at its own time and temperature.*

## 1. WHAT WE ASK FOR
One complete machine, built turnkey to this specification: design, manufacturing, factory acceptance test, delivery to Istanbul, installation support, training, documentation and spare parts. If the first unit works, further units will follow.

We have already designed and simulated the line (layout, station sequence, cycle times). We are looking for a manufacturer to build it.

## 2. CAPACITY
- Peak output: **55 finished products per hour**
- Daily output: **280 products** (about 200 thin + 80 thick)
- Operation: 12–14 hours per day, 7 days per week
- Unmanned during operation; one person refills the machine once a day

## 3. PRODUCT
- Round flatbread, **Ø300 mm**
- Two types: **thin** (about 1,5 mm after pressing) and **thick** (about 3 mm)
- Dough balls are bought ready and stored cold; **110 g** (thin) and **220 g** (thick)
- After baking the product is cut into slices, lightly oil-sprayed, packed in a **320 × 320 × 45 mm** box
- One **0,33 L drink can** may be added to the same order

## 4. MACHINE PRINCIPLE
**There is no conveyor in the machine.** All process stations are fixed and the product is carried only by the two robots. This is a deliberate decision: it keeps the machine short, and it lets each product bake at its own time and temperature.

## 5. STATIONS (in process order)
1. **COLD STORE** — refrigerated motorised drawers at +3 °C. 3 days of dough (30 drawers, drawer size about 620 × 680 mm) and 4 drawers of drink cans. Drawer opens and presents the front row to the robot; staff refills from the outside.
2. **PRESS** — heated press, flattens the dough ball to Ø300 mm at the two thicknesses above.
3. **TOPPING** — fixed dosing heads; the robot holds the pressed base under the heads and rotates it while the ingredient is deposited. 5 ingredients:
   - thick raw minced-meat paste, **110 g spread evenly over the whole Ø300 surface**
   - grated cheese, 90–130 g
   - diced meat (about 10 mm cubes), 145 g
   - sliced sausage, 70 g, sliced from a bar of Ø38 mm into 3 mm slices
   - minced-meat filling, 160 g
   Accuracy **±5 %** of target weight, even distribution, no empty spots, about 15 mm free edge.
   All ingredients are kept at +3 °C. Ingredient containers must be removable for daily cleaning.
4. **OVENS — cavity type, no conveyor.** Total **3 cavities**, loaded and unloaded by the robot, each with a motorised lid, stone hearth, top and bottom heating, 300–350 °C:
   - **2 cavities for the thin product**, one above the other in one body, bake about 120 s
   - **1 cavity for the thick product** in a second body, bake about 240 s at its own temperature
   Bake times will be confirmed by test. Each cavity must be controlled independently (own time and temperature). Please state the cavity size you can offer; a cavity able to take **two Ø300 products side by side** is welcome.
5. **CUT AND SPRAY** — light oil spray and cutting into 4–6 slices.
6. **PACK** — box magazine, box opening/forming, closing the lid.
7. **PICKUP** — 12 heated lockers on the customer side; the customer opens the locker with a QR code.

## 6. ROBOTS
Two 6-axis collaborative robots, payload 5 kg including gripper, mounted on the machine body at about 1.400 mm height. **No floor rail, no ceiling rail.**
- **Robot 1** — takes the dough ball from the drawer, places it on the press, then holds the pressed base under the dosing heads. About 58 s per product.
- **Robot 2** — loads and unloads the oven cavities, feeds cutting and packing, places the closed box and the drink can into the locker. About 47 s per product.
Pick and place heights are between **600 mm and 1.700 mm** from the floor.
We can supply the robots ourselves (Fairino FR5 / FR5 WML) or you may propose your own. Grippers must be food-safe.

## 7. MACHINE BODY
- Modular cabinets, **depth 830 mm**, **height 1.915 mm**, total length about **6.000 mm**
- Robot corridor in front: 800 mm
- 304 stainless steel, food grade, daily wipe cleaning
- Removable parts (ingredient containers, trays) must fit a 500 × 500 mm dishwasher basket
- We will design the outer skin ourselves; the machine may be delivered without decorative panels
